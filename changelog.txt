Quark Woven 4.0-463.woven.1, Zeta Woven 1.0-30.woven.1, Quark Woven Oddities 1.0.0

First release: an unofficial Fabric port of Quark 4.0-463 for Minecraft 1.20.1 (upstream commit c1bb062), with the
same content as the Forge version.

* Needs Zeta Woven, Fabric API (0.92.9 or newer, so Fabric Loader 0.16.10 or newer) and Forge Config API Port.
  Quark Woven Oddities is optional and turns on the Oddities content (backpacks, crates, pipes, magnets, matrix
  enchanting, totem of holding, tiny potato), like Quark Oddities on Forge. Mod Menu is optional.
* Zeta Woven is Zeta 1.0-29.135 (the version Quark 4.0-463 is built against) plus the fix from Zeta 1.0-30: an item
  from another mod that throws an exception when asked for its crafting remainder no longer breaks Zeta's recipe
  handling.
* Differences from Quark on Forge:
  * no integrations with other mods yet (JEI, Lootr, TerraBlender, land-claim mods);
  * wall hanging signs made of Quark wood drop hanging signs (they drop regular signs on Forge, an upstream bug);
  * recipe viewers may show stale ingredients for config-dependent recipes until the game restarts.
* Tested on a dedicated server and over LAN, and with Sodium and Iris (including a shader pack).
